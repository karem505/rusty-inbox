An optional AI assistant inside your mail, with the provider and the model you choose. It is off until you turn it on, and nothing changes in your draft until you click Replace.

▶ **Watch it in 100 seconds:** [Rusty Inbox 0.2.0 on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tx4bGmOI94c) (real recording on a fictional demo mailbox, with real model answers). The full story: [an email client where you choose the AI](https://aboelmakarem.pro/blog/rusty-inbox-ai-email-assistant-chatgpt-ollama).

### Choose your AI (Settings → AI)
- **Sign in with your ChatGPT subscription.** No API key: click *Sign in with ChatGPT*, approve in the browser, done (a device code works too when the browser cannot reach the app).
- **Or bring a key:** OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Ollama (on this computer or ollama.com) or any OpenAI-compatible server (LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp…).
- **The model list comes live from the provider**, with search, so new models show up the day they ship. Pick a **thinking level** for the models that support one.
- *Test connection* answers with the model's reply and the time it took.

### Writing
- **Proofread:** fixes spelling, grammar and punctuation only, with the changes highlighted.
- **Rewrite:** formal, friendly, shorter, longer, clearer or more persuasive.
- **Translate** your draft (English, Arabic or any language you type).
- **Write for me:** a short instruction or a few bullet points become the full email, using the conversation you reply to.
- **Suggest a subject:** three options to pick from.
- Your signature and the quoted message stay untouched. The buttons sit next to *Send* (and `Ctrl+J`).

### Reading
- **Summary** of the conversation with its **action items** and due dates (`Shift+S`).
- **Translate** a message in place, and switch back to the original.
- **Three suggested replies:** click one to open the reply with it.
- **Attachment summary** for PDF, DOCX, text, CSV, HTML and .eml files, without opening them.
- **Phishing check:** a risk level with the reasons (sender vs Reply-To, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, where links really go, urgency).
- It knows which addresses are yours: mail you sent from any of your accounts is summarized as yours, and the suggested replies are your follow-ups, not answers to yourself.

### Organizing (each one off until you switch it on)
- **Smart filtering:** new mail the rules are unsure about is sorted by the model.
- **Needs reply:** mail that expects an answer from you gets a mark and its own list.
- **Follow up:** mail you sent that asked for something and got no answer after a few days.
- **Ask in plain words:** type `?` and a question in the search field ("invoices from Cloudhost last month"); it becomes a search you can still edit.
- **Daily briefing** at the hour you choose: people first, then what needs action, then the rest in one line each.

### Privacy
- Nothing is sent until you choose a provider. The text you act on goes to that provider only; background helpers look only at mail that arrives after you switch them on.
- Accounts you exclude are never sent, by any feature. With Ollama on this computer, nothing leaves it.
- Keys and the ChatGPT sign-in stay in the local secret store with your mail passwords.

### Changes
- **Flatpak is now the main Linux package:** the README and the one-line installer put it first. An existing AppImage or tarball install keeps updating the way it was installed.

### Fixes
- The app could crash when you closed or discarded a draft while it was saving (Qt 6.12).

### Install
**Linux: Flatpak is the recommended package** (signed, sandboxed, any distribution, updates with `flatpak update`):

```sh
flatpak install --user https://karem505.github.io/rusty-inbox/rusty-inbox.flatpakref
```

Already installed: `flatpak update`. The one-line installer now picks Flatpak too: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/karem505/rusty-inbox/main/scripts/install.sh | sh`.

- **Linux, other options:** the AppImage, `.flatpak` bundle or tarball below.
- **Windows:** `setup.exe` or the portable zip.
- **macOS:** `.dmg` for Apple Silicon (arm64) or Intel (x86_64).
- **Already on 0.1.2 or later:** Settings → Updates.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
