Push that stays connected, and far fewer connections to your mail server.

### Fewer connections (hosts that limit them per IP, such as cPanel)
- **Accounts with push skip the every-minute full check.** Their Inbox is pushed already, so the other folders (Sent, Archive, changes made on another device) are checked every 10 minutes instead. "Check for new mail" and sending a message still check every account at once.
- **Pushed mail reuses the push connection.** New mail and changes the server announces are synced over the connection that was already waiting, with no extra login.
- **No spare connections for accounts with push.** The app now holds one connection per account (it held two or three before). With three accounts on one cPanel server, that is 3 connections instead of 6 to 8.
- New logins to the same server are at least 20 seconds apart (12 before), so the startup no longer trips the server's login limit.

### Push that does not go quiet
- A push connection that a firewall dropped without telling either side used to look alive until the next renewal, about 9 to 11 minutes later. Mail that arrived in the meantime waited for the next check. TCP keepalive now notices a dead connection within about two minutes, IDLE is renewed every 5 minutes, and the app reconnects and catches up by itself.

Measured on a cPanel (Dovecot) account: new mail is in the app **0.5 s** after it reaches the server. Gmail can take longer to announce mail you send to yourself; the app syncs within a couple of seconds of Gmail's notice.

### Install
- **Flatpak (self-hosted, signed):** `flatpak install --user https://karem505.github.io/rusty-inbox/rusty-inbox.flatpakref`. Already installed: `flatpak update`.
- **Linux:** AppImage, `.flatpak` bundle or tarball below, or `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/karem505/rusty-inbox/main/scripts/install.sh | sh`.
- **Windows:** `setup.exe` or the portable zip.
- **macOS:** `.dmg` for Apple Silicon (arm64) or Intel (x86_64).
- **Already on 0.1.2 or later:** Settings → Updates.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
