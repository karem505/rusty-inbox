# Rusty Inbox: launch motion video

## Style Prompt
Dark, warm, precise product launch. A near-black warm canvas with a soft rust glow, cream type, and one copper accent. Real app footage sits in floating, slightly tilted windows with deep soft shadows. Motion is fast and confident (expo/power eases, 0.5-0.8 s entrances), never bouncy or cartoonish. One idea per scene: a kicker, a short headline, one line of body copy, the footage.

## Colors
- Canvas: #0E0D0C (background), #191715 (cards / window chrome)
- Ink: #F6EEE6 (headlines), #C9BEB3 (body, passes AA on the canvas)
- Accent: #E8743B (rust/copper, from the app icon); deep accent #B7410E (glows, bars)
- Hairline: rgba(246,238,230,0.10)

## Typography
- Thmanyah Sans (Black for headlines, Bold for kickers and numbers, Regular for body). Local files in assets/fonts.

## What NOT to Do
- No full-screen linear gradients (banding); use radial glows.
- No fake UI: every app screen is real footage from the recorded demo.
- No real personal data; the demo mailbox is fictional.
- No other products' names or logos (including Rust's official logo and Ferris).
- No bouncy/elastic eases; no infinite repeats.
