# Rusty Inbox 0.2.0: voiceover script

Voice: ElevenLabs "Daniel - Steady Broadcaster", model eleven_v4. One line per sentence (each is synthesized whole).

## 1. Hook
- Rusty Inbox zero point two point zero.
- Your AI. Your model. Your mail.

## 2. Choose your AI
- Sign in with your ChatGPT subscription, with no API key.
- Or use OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Ollama, or any compatible server.
- The model list comes live from your provider, with search, and you pick how hard it thinks.

## 3. Write
- Proofread shows every change, highlighted.
- Rewrite it friendlier, or shorter.
- Or give it one line, and let it write the message.
- Nothing changes until you click Replace, and your signature and the quoted mail stay untouched.

## 4. Read
- One click summarizes a thread, with action items and due dates.
- Pick one of three suggested replies, and it opens the reply for you.
- Translate in place. Summarize an attachment.

## 5. Stay safe
- Check for phishing, and see the risk level and the reasons.

## 6. Stay on top
- Needs reply lists the mail waiting on you.
- Follow up remembers what nobody answered.
- Ask in plain words, and get a search you can edit.
- And a daily briefing starts your morning.

## 7. Privacy
- It is off until you turn it on.
- Only the mail you act on goes to the provider you chose, you can exclude accounts, and with Ollama it all stays on your machine.

## 8. Outro
- Rusty Inbox zero point two. Free and open source, for Linux, Windows and macOS.
- Install it with Flatpak, and find the code on GitHub.
